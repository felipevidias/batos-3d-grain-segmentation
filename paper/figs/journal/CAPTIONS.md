# Journal figures — draft captions

Generated by `scripts/make_journal_figures.py` (read-only on existing run outputs; matplotlib; 300 dpi). Masters are in
`C:\BA_TOS_DATA\figures\`, with the per-figure numbers next to them (`fig1_f1.csv`, `fig2_info.json`, `fig4_scale.csv`,
`fig5_volume_error.csv`, `fig6_info.json`). "v2" = `batos-paper-v2-frozen` (SIBGRAPI), "v2-fixflat" = v2 with the

settings.

**Fig. 1 — `fig1_qualitative_slices.png`.**
Central xy slice (z = 100) of four 200³ crops:
- EFRGP01_00_crop04: development crop.
- EFRGP01_00_crop05: development crop containing the membrane and exterior air.
- EMRGP01_00_test01 and EORGP01_00_test01: interior test crops of two other specimens.

Columns: grey value, ground truth, BA-TOS v2, v2-fixflat, v3, Cellpose-SAM v2. Each GT grain has a random colour. A
prediction matched to a GT grain (3D IoU > 0.5) takes that grain's colour. An unmatched prediction gets its own colour.
GT grains excluded from evaluation (touching a crop face or < 1000 voxels), and predictions lying in them, are drawn
desaturated. The yellow box marks the 48 × 48 window where the methods disagree most with the GT on this slice; the
inset magnifies it.

Under each panel: F1@0.50 for the whole 3D crop, no size filter, under the no-marker evaluation (a prediction is ignored
if ≥ 50 % of it lies in excluded GT; the mode used for the Cellpose comparison). These values therefore differ slightly
from the seed-ignore numbers in Tables II/III (e.g. v2 on crop04: 0.980 here vs 0.985).

What the panels show:
- v2 collapses on crop05 (0.213), where exterior air breaks the flatten offset (Figs. 2–3).
- v2 and v2-fixflat merge the EMR crop into one label (0.000).
- v2-fixflat produced no output on EORGP01_00_test01 (run failed, scored 0).
- v3 is close to Cellpose-SAM v2 on the EF and EM crops. It under-seeds the larger EO grains (0.544).

**Fig. 2 — `fig2_crop05_orthogonal_markers.png`.**
EFRGP01_00_crop05, the crop on which v2's support-collapse gate fires. Shown are the three central orthogonal slices
(xy, xz, yz) through the crop centre.

Overlays:
- **Green:** valid GT boundaries.
- **Orange:** excluded GT.
- **Magenta:** the confining membrane: bright Otsu foreground carrying no GT label, one connected sheet.
- **Blue:** exterior air, the largest dark connected region. It is 51 % of the crop.

Dots are ToS-MIN markers within ±3 voxels of each plane:
- **Red:** v2, 53 markers in the crop. They sit in inter-grain pores and in air. The gate then replaces them with
  EDT-Otsu markers.
- **Cyan rings:** v3, 188 markers, about one per grain, at the grain cores.

The membrane and air are drawn for illustration only (GT is used here as a display mask, never by the method).

**Fig. 3 — `fig3_crop05_flatten_offset.png`.**
The selector flatten (λ = 1) on EFRGP01_00_crop05, central xy slice, with intensity histograms below (log density):
- Green: voxels inside GT grains.
- Grey: everything else.
- Black outline: the grains of crop04 under the frozen flatten, for reference.

Left, the frozen flatten. About half the crop is exterior air with an opening-by-reconstruction value of 0, so its
background reference, median(blur[blur > 0]), falls to 0.9 (crop04: 186). The grain bodies lose their offset (grain
median 43; the crop04 grain histogram peaks near 160) and merge with pores and air in the dark range where ToS-MIN candidates are sought.

Right, the fixed flatten: bg_ref = median(blur ≥ 1) = 176.7. The grain histogram returns to the crop04 reference
(median 164), and v2-fixflat reaches F1@0.50 = 0.98–0.99 on this crop.

**Fig. 4 — `fig4_scale_estimates.png`.**
GT-free grain-radius estimates against the GT median inscribed radius, for all 64 crops (12 development + 52 test),
coloured by specimen; squares are development, circles are test.
- Left, v3's r_granulo: all 64 within ±10 % (median relative error −5 %).
- Right, v2's e13 estimate r̂: 19/63 within ±10 % (median −25 %). It underestimates by ~50 % on the larger-grained EM and
  EO specimens. e13 failed on EOSGP01_00_test03, so n = 63.

The GT radius is used for diagnosis only. Both quantities are discrete, so a horizontal jitter of ±0.08 voxel was added
for display.

**Fig. 5 — `fig5_boundary_contours.png`.**
Boundary fidelity on a 64 × 64 window of the central slice of EFRGP01_00_crop04 (left) and EORGP01_00_test01 (right).
- White dashed: GT.
- Cyan: BA-TOS v3.
- Magenta: Cellpose-SAM v2.

Only grains matched (IoU > 0.5) by both methods are drawn. Cellpose-SAM v2 contours sit about one voxel outside the GT
all around each grain. Over all grains matched by both:
- crop04 (259 grains): median relative volume error +11.7 % for Cellpose-SAM v2 vs −3.7 % for v3.
- EOR test01 (69 grains): +9.4 % vs +6.4 %.

This is consistent with the ≈ 1-voxel outward bias (+8 to +11 % volume) reported in the shape analysis.

**Fig. 6 — `fig6_smoke_block_3d.png`.**
Full-volume tiled BA-TOS v3 (smoke test `smoke_center3`, central 3 × 3 × 3 tiles of EFRGP01_00, 600³ core, halo 20,
orphan-rescue stitching). The block holds 9,753 instances. On the 6,005 valid GT grains it scores F1@0.50 = 0.865,
seed-ignore, no filter.

(a) Three faces of the block with one colour per grain, cut 30 voxels inside the core faces. Instances are owned by
their centroid's tile, and no tiles exist outside this block, so the outermost shell is half empty. The black bands
along the vertical edges are exterior air: the block's corners lie outside the cylindrical specimen (radius ≈ 349
voxels, corners ≈ 390 voxels from its axis). The GT is empty there too.

(b) Marching-cubes surfaces (step 2) of the 55 grains whose centroid lies in a central 100³ sub-block. Lambert
shading, one colour per grain.
