# Journal figures — draft captions

Generated by `scripts/make_journal_figures.py` (read-only on existing run outputs; matplotlib; 300 dpi). Masters are in
`C:\BA_TOS_DATA\figures\`, with the per-figure numbers next to them (`fig1_f1.csv`, `fig2_info.json`, `fig4_scale.csv`,
`fig5_volume_error.csv`, `fig5_volume_error_per_grain.csv`, `fig6_info.json`).

Method names used in all figures and captions (run/config identifiers in brackets):
- **BA-TOS [SIBGRAPI'26]**: the SIBGRAPI method as frozen (`batos-paper-v2-frozen`).
- **BA-TOS + corrected flattening**: the same method with the corrected flatten offset (`batos-paper-v2-fixflat`).
- **BA-TOS-SF (proposed)**: the scale-free journal method (`batos-paper-v3-scalefree`).
- **Cellpose-SAM**: `cpsam_v2`, default settings.

**Fig. 1 — `fig1_qualitative_slices.png`.**
Central xy slice (z = 100) of four 200³ crops:
- EFRGP01_00_crop04: development crop.
- EFRGP01_00_crop05: development crop containing the membrane and exterior air.
- EMRGP01_00_test01 and EORGP01_00_test01: interior test crops of two other specimens.

Columns: grey value, ground truth, BA-TOS [SIBGRAPI'26], BA-TOS + corrected flattening, BA-TOS-SF (proposed),
Cellpose-SAM. Each GT grain has a random colour. A prediction matched to a GT grain (3D IoU > 0.5) takes that grain's
colour. An unmatched prediction gets its own colour. GT grains excluded from evaluation (touching a crop face or < 1000
voxels), and predictions lying in them, are drawn desaturated.

The yellow box marks a 48 × 48 window that the inset magnifies:
- On crop05 the window is centred on the membrane edge. It is the window, among those centred on that edge, with the
  most pixels where BA-TOS [SIBGRAPI'26] is wrong (y 105–153, x 15–63). The membrane is the display mask of Fig. 2, and
  GT is used for display only.
- On the other crops it is the window where the four methods disagree most with the GT on this slice.

Under each panel: F1@0.50 for the whole 3D crop, no size filter, under the no-marker evaluation. In that evaluation a
prediction is ignored if ≥ 50 % of it lies in excluded GT; it is the mode used for the Cellpose comparison. These values
therefore differ slightly from the seed-ignore numbers in Tables II/III (e.g. BA-TOS [SIBGRAPI'26] on crop04: 0.980
here vs 0.985).

What the panels show:
- BA-TOS [SIBGRAPI'26] collapses on crop05 (0.213), where exterior air breaks the flatten offset (Figs. 2–3). Along the
  membrane edge (inset) it merges and mislabels the grains that the other three methods separate.
- BA-TOS [SIBGRAPI'26] and BA-TOS + corrected flattening merge the EMR crop into one label (0.000).
- BA-TOS + corrected flattening produced no output on EORGP01_00_test01 (run failed, scored 0).
- BA-TOS-SF (proposed) is close to Cellpose-SAM on the EF and EM crops. It under-seeds the larger EO grains (0.544).

**Fig. 2 — `fig2_crop05_orthogonal_markers.png`.**
EFRGP01_00_crop05, the crop on which the support-collapse gate of BA-TOS [SIBGRAPI'26] fires. Shown are the three
central orthogonal slices (xy, xz, yz) through the crop centre.

Overlays:
- **Green:** valid GT boundaries.
- **Orange:** excluded GT.
- **Magenta:** the confining membrane: bright Otsu foreground carrying no GT label, one connected sheet.
- **Blue:** exterior air, the largest dark connected region. It is 51 % of the crop.

Dots are ToS-MIN markers within ±3 voxels of each plane:
- **Red:** BA-TOS [SIBGRAPI'26], 53 markers in the crop. They sit in inter-grain pores and in air. The gate then
  replaces them with EDT-Otsu markers.
- **Cyan rings:** BA-TOS-SF (proposed), 188 markers, about one per grain, at the grain cores.

The membrane and air are drawn for illustration only (GT is used here as a display mask, never by the method).

**Fig. 3 — `fig3_crop05_flatten_offset.png`.**
The selector flatten (λ = 1) on EFRGP01_00_crop05, central xy slice, with intensity histograms below (log density):
- Green: voxels inside GT grains.
- Grey: everything else.
- Black outline: the grains of crop04 under the frozen flatten, for reference.

Left, the flatten of BA-TOS [SIBGRAPI'26]. About half the crop is exterior air with an opening-by-reconstruction value
of 0, so its background reference, median(blur[blur > 0]), falls to 0.9 (crop04: 186). The grain bodies lose their
offset (grain median 43; the crop04 grain histogram peaks near 160). They merge with pores and air in the dark range
where ToS-MIN candidates are sought.

Right, the corrected flatten: bg_ref = median(blur ≥ 1) = 176.7. The grain histogram returns to the crop04 reference
(median 164), and BA-TOS + corrected flattening reaches F1@0.50 = 0.98–0.99 on this crop.

**Fig. 4 — `fig4_scale_estimates.png`.**
GT-free grain-radius estimates against the GT median inscribed radius, for all 64 crops (12 development + 52 test),
coloured by specimen; squares are development, circles are test.
- Left, r_granulo of BA-TOS-SF (proposed): all 64 within ±10 % (median relative error −5 %).
- Right, the e13 estimate r̂ of BA-TOS [SIBGRAPI'26]: 19/63 within ±10 % (median −25 %). It underestimates by ~50 % on
  the larger-grained EM and EO specimens. e13 failed on EOSGP01_00_test03, so n = 63.

The GT radius is used for diagnosis only. Both quantities are discrete, so a horizontal jitter of ±0.08 voxel was added
for display.

**Fig. 5 — `fig5_boundary_contours.png`.**
Boundary fidelity of BA-TOS-SF (proposed) and Cellpose-SAM. Only grains matched (3D IoU > 0.5) by both methods and
valid under the evaluation protocol are used.

(a, b) Contours on a 32 × 32 window of the central slice (z = 100) of EFRGP01_00_crop04 (y 26–58, x 144–176) and
EORGP01_00_test01 (y 95–127, x 120–152):
- White dashed: GT.
- Cyan: BA-TOS-SF (proposed).
- Magenta: Cellpose-SAM.

The window is the one covering the most such grains. Cellpose-SAM contours sit about one voxel outside the GT all
around each grain.

(c) Histogram of the per-grain relative volume error (predicted volume / GT volume − 1), in 2 % bins, as a fraction of
grains:
- Solid lines: crop04 (259 grains). Dashed lines: EOR test01 (69 grains).
- Vertical lines mark the medians. Values above +60 % (18 grains, all BA-TOS-SF) are drawn in the last bin.

Medians:

| Crop | BA-TOS-SF (proposed) | Cellpose-SAM |
|---|---|---|
| crop04 | −3.7 % | +11.7 % |
| EOR test01 | +6.4 % | +9.4 % |

Spread differs as much as the medians do:
- Cellpose-SAM is a narrow, consistently positive band: +9.7 to +14.3 % on crop04, +6.1 to +12.3 % on EOR test01. This
  matches the ≈ 1-voxel outward bias (+8 to +11 % volume) reported in the shape analysis.
- BA-TOS-SF (proposed) is centred nearer zero on crop04 (IQR −4.1 to +5.3 %) but has a heavy over-volume tail:
  - crop04: 46/259 grains above +20 %, max +79 %;
  - EOR test01: 28/69 above +20 % (IQR +2.2 to +39 %), max +91 %.

**Fig. 6 — `fig6_smoke_block_3d.png`.**
Full-volume tiled BA-TOS-SF (proposed): smoke test `smoke_center3`, the central 3 × 3 × 3 tiles of EFRGP01_00. The
core is 600³, with a halo of 20 and orphan-rescue stitching. The block holds 9,753 instances. On the 6,005 valid GT
grains it scores F1@0.50 = 0.865, seed-ignore, no filter.

(a) Three faces of the block with one colour per grain, cut 30 voxels inside the core faces. Instances are owned by
their centroid's tile, and no tiles exist outside this block, so the outermost shell is half empty. The black bands
along the vertical edges are exterior air: the block's corners lie outside the cylindrical specimen (radius ≈ 349
voxels, corners ≈ 390 voxels from its axis). The GT is empty there too.

(b) Marching-cubes surfaces (step 2) of the 55 grains whose centroid lies in a central 100³ sub-block. Lambert
shading, one colour per grain.
